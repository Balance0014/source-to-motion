# GapMine art plate prompts

The image plates were made with the built-in image generation tool. They are concept artwork, not screenshots of GapMine or factual visualizations. The scene code adds all text, numbers, camera movement, particles, and light pulses.

## Market landscape, revealed (`assets/gapmine-market-lit.png`)

> Premium cinematic motion-design keyframe, vertical 4:5. A colossal tangible market landscape of densely packed dark graphite glass cells and towers. One immense unexplored gap cuts through the center; molten amber light shines from deep inside it while fine cyan source streams converge. Low oblique aerial perspective, foreground occlusion, atmospheric depth, volumetric lighting, high contrast, dark perimeter for later dashboard data. No text, numbers, logos, UI, charts, people, globe, or planets.

## Market landscape, before discovery (`assets/gapmine-market-dark.png`)

> Edit the revealed landscape while preserving its camera angle, tower geometry, canyon position, and composition. Darken the canyon to near-black with only faint buried warmth; reduce cyan streams to traces. Keep the same structures so the plates can transition from hidden gap to revealed opportunity. No text, data, UI, or new objects.

The 4:5 mobile dashboard composition uses these matching plates in `scene.py`. Their market terrain is a metaphor. Numeric panels and captions are drawn separately from the dated `facts.json` manifest.
