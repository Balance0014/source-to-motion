# GapMine · scattered demand becoming a sourced opportunity

**Industry read.** The public [GapMine homepage](https://gapmine.com/) describes builder posts, GitHub issues, and product reviews as sources; it presents a five-signal model and source-linked opportunity cards. `source.json` is a dated public homepage snapshot, so changing counters in the film are labeled as snapshot values.

**Input → mechanism → output.** Many scattered builder signals → source threads converge and five named demand dimensions assemble → a specific source-linked opportunity appears.

**Direction choice.** The original market-canyon art provides a concrete terrain. Instead of repeating a fixed four-sided HUD, this scene sends moving evidence threads into a widening physical gap, then reveals the five dimensions around the convergence point and finally brings the opportunity card into the foreground. A generic storm was rejected because it would hide the product's source-to-opportunity logic.

**Evidence and boundary.** The landscape, flow, and positions are metaphorical. The film does not display an invented score, trend, or simulated counter. The **101,081** signals, **124** communities, **1,140** cards, and featured opportunity text are from the captured homepage, not live values.
